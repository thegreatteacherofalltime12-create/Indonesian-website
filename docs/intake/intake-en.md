## Key facts (verified) that back this up

**What US buyers face with these products**
- Aluminium-frame outdoor seating with synthetic rattan is metal furniture for US customs: main codes are MFN duty-free plus the 10% Section 301 tariff in force since 2026-07-24; the 25% Section 232 tariff applies only to upholstered wooden seats. Outdoor-only cushions are exempt from the 16 CFR 1640 flammability rule. — hts.usitc.gov (2026-09-16); USTR (2026-07-23/28); CBP CSMS (2025-10-10); cpsc.gov.
- Natural rattan and bamboo seating and furniture (9401.52/.53/.59, 9403.82/.83/.89) require a Lacey Act declaration with genus and species per component on formal entries (Phase VII, 2024-12-01). — aphis.usda.gov/plant-imports/file-lacey-act-declaration (2026-01-06).
- Sonokeling/rosewood (Dalbergia spp.) is CITES Appendix II with only a 10 kg-per-shipment finished-product exemption; commercial import needs a USDA permit. — CITES CoP20 documents; aphis.usda.gov/plant-imports/cites (2026-09-09).
- Wood packaging without the ISPM-15 mark is refused, re-exported or destroyed at the importer's cost. — aphis.usda.gov/plant-imports/wood-packaging-material/import (2026-07-27).
- Indonesia–US ocean transit: FCL 27–39 days, LCL 30–42 days (indicative). — freightos.com (Sept 2026).

**Indonesian export side**
- A V-Legal document (SVLK) is required for wood-furniture export customs clearance; whether rattan-only products are carved out is unresolved. Which entity's SVLK covers a shipment arranged by an agent is a question for the agent and the manufacturer, not for the website. — mongabay.com (2020-05-27), timbertradeportal.com (2020-06-09). **Not independently re-verified.**
