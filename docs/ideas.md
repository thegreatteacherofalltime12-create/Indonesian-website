## Seasonal / special events ("Spesial Event")

Proposed 2026-09-25: a seasonal section on the site — Christmas first, other
themes later. Parked until the products and photos are ready; likely built as
a category or featured collection rather than a separate page.

The collection so far (~22 items, priced privately): driftwood trees, turned
and fur-fabric animal figures, dressed ducks, candle holders in teak, stone
and mixed woods, and pinecone decorations. Seasonal buyers order around
March–June, so nothing is lost by waiting for confirmed products.
