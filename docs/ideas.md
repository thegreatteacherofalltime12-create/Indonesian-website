## Seasonal / special events ("Spesial Event")

Proposed 2026-09-25: a seasonal section on the site — Christmas first, other
themes later. Parked until products, prices and photos are ready; likely built
as a category or a featured collection rather than a separate page.

Christmas theme, seen so far (photos to be saved to `client-docs/ideas/christmas-2026/`):

- **Driftwood Christmas trees** — teak-branch trees on natural teak-root bases,
  three sizes shown standing together (roughly knee to chest height).
- **Felt Santa / gnome ornaments** — wooden-ball heads with felt hats, red and
  green variants, hanging on twine loops. Handmade, very small, likely sold in sets.
- **Turned-wood reindeer figures** — two styles: all-wood turned deer with cut
  antlers, and a rounder figure with brass antlers. (Some photos may be
  reference/inspiration images rather than their own production.)
- **Candle holders** — round stone/concrete tea-light holders, and turned wood
  tea-light holders in graded finishes. (Also possibly reference images.)

Open before this goes on the site: which items are their own production vs
inspiration, prices, sizes, materials (wood species for Lacey/EUDR), lead time
for seasonal ordering (buyers order Christmas stock around March–June).
